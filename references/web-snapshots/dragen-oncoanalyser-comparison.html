DRAGEN ⟂ oncoanalyser

Same four tumor / normal pairs from Sid's data, two pipelines. The DRAGEN Somatic 4.4.6001 pipeline runs end- to-end on Illumina BaseSpace; oncoanalyser runs the Hartwig Medical Foundation tool stack via nf-core. They share the same input WGS data but use different callers, different annotation, and different driver-scoring. This page is where they agree, where they diverge, and what each surfaces alone.

Headline: the two pipelines agree on the basic biology (purity, ploidy, MSS, TMB, the 12q amplicon, the T2 TGFBR2 multi-hit), but disagree sharply on HRD. They also produce different SNV / driver lists for reasons that are mostly about how each pipeline filters, not what's in the data.

Where they agree

  • Purity within ± 0.02 across all four pairs — independent fits land within rounding error (DRAGEN 0.63 / 0.34 / 0.14 / 0.32 vs oncoanalyser 0.65 / 0.36 / 0.16 / 0.34).
  • Ploidy ≈ 3.1–3.2, whole-genome doubled, in all four pairs.
  • MSS in all four pairs (MSI < 7.5% per DRAGEN, MSS / 0.03–0.05 indels-per-Mb per oncoanalyser).
  • TMB-low in the patient samples (1.3–4.1 mut/Mb). Both pipelines flag the organoid as much higher than the patient samples — DRAGEN reports 360 mut/Mb (variant counts), oncoanalyser reports 2.6 mut/Mb (different denominator after subclonal + filter pruning).
  • The CDK4 / MDM2 12q amplicon at CN ≈ 7 in every pair. Note both pipelines show this only as AMP, not HIGH_AMP — strict ≥ 3 × ploidy thresholds miss this in the triploid background.
  • The T2 TGFBR2 multi-hit is a clean cross-pipeline confirmation. DRAGEN flagged 5 events on TGFBR2 (1 BND + 2 DEL + 2 INS); oncoanalyser's LINX driver catalog calls it HOM_DEL_DISRUPTION (biallelic, likelihood 1.0) and the same TGFBR2–IGHG1 BND shows up.
  • Same HLA genotype across all four pairs (DRAGEN HLA and oncoanalyser LILAC agree on A*01:01 hom, B*08:01 / B*27:05, C*01:02 / C*07:01).

Where they disagree — and why

HRD: HRD-positive vs HR-proficient

DRAGEN reports the Telli/Myriad-style HRD score (LOH + TAI + LST = 31–45) for every pair, all in the HRD-positive range (≥ 42 = high). oncoanalyser's CHORD classifier calls all four pairs HR-proficient (p_hrd 0.014–0.076, well below the 0.5 deficiency cutoff).

These are different methods measuring different things. DRAGEN counts large-scale break footprints — a structural-instability signal. CHORD is a logistic regression trained on BRCA1 / BRCA2-deficient samples, looking specifically for the indel + SBS3 + SV-type signature characteristic of HR deficiency. A genome can carry the structural-instability footprint (DRAGEN+) without satisfying the canonical BRCAness pattern (CHORD−). The SIGS allocation tells the same story — Sig3 is dominant (15–31%) but the model isn't sure enough to certify HRD.

Practical reading: don't rely on either as a sole indicator. The SIGS Sig3 allocation + DRAGEN's HRD score together support "structural instability with BRCAness flavor"; CHORD's HR-proficient call argues against acting on it as a PARP-target rationale. Reconcile against the BRCA1 / BRCA2 / RAD51 status directly if it matters clinically.

Driver lists are computing different things

The driver-overlap table looks dramatic but isn't really a disagreement — the two pipelines define "driver" differently:

  • oncoanalyser: the PURPLE + LINX driver catalog — only events that pass HMF's likelihood-scored thresholds (typically 5–13 entries per pair).
  • DRAGEN here: approximated as "any CGC tier-1 gene hit by a HIGH/MODERATE SNV, a CNV, or a SV" (60–90 genes per pair).

DRAGEN's cast a much wider net. Of the 3 driver genes flagged by both pipelines across all four pairs:

  • TGFBR2 in T2 (ucla_T2)
  • CNOT3 in T1 organoid (ucla_T1_organoid)
  • KMT2A in T1 organoid (ucla_T1_organoid)

The pairs where DRAGEN-only catches more (e.g. T2 with 90 dragen-only drivers) reflect the wide CGC net, not missing biology in oncoanalyser. The other direction — five genes flagged by oncoanalyser that DRAGEN misses (T2 FGFR3, H1-2, SPRED1, KMT2C, CDKN2B) — is more interesting: these are PURPLE / LINX driver scores that DRAGEN's panel doesn't surface as tier-1 + functional, despite the variants being there.
